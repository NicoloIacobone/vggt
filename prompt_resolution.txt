CONTEXT
- My model: a MaskDINO-style decoder on top of a VGGT backbone, for 3D semantic/instance segmentation on ScanNet scenes.

- MaskDINO's pixel decoder expects a multi-scale feature pyramid (analogous to ResNet C3/C4/C5) and produces a high-resolution "mask feature" map used for the per-query mask logits. Right now I only have one resolution (37x37), so my output masks are capped at that resolution.

GOAL
Get from VGGT's single-scale 37x37 tokens to a multi-scale pyramid + a high-resolution mask feature map suitable for MaskDINO, without abandoning VGGT's pretrained geometry-aware features.

APPROACHES TO EVALUATE (from prior research on VGGT-based segmentation papers — PanSt3R, EPS3D, UNITE, VGGT-Ω, VGGT-Segmentor):
